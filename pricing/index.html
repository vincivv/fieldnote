Questions, answered.

What counts as a conversation?

One call, one ticket thread, one chat or one review. A 40-message ticket is still one conversation.

Do you train models on our data?

No. Your conversations are used only to build your workspace, and you can delete everything with one click.

How long does setup take?

Most teams connect their first source in under five minutes and get a first brief the same morning.

Can we switch plans later?

Yes, at any time. Upgrades apply right away; downgrades take effect at the end of the billing period.
